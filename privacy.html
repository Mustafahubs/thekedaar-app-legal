Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Thekedaar App ("the app") is a record-keeping tool for contractors. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details: your WhatsApp/phone number; if you choose to link them, your email address and Google account email and basic profile (name, email). We request only the basic Google sign-in scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • Business records you enter: workers, attendance, advances, settlements, projects, rates, measurements, bills, notes, and optional worker photos and voice notes.
  • Device information: phone model, Android version and app version, used to keep only one active login per account.

How we use it

To sign you in, keep your records on your phone and back them up and sync them to our cloud database (Supabase), and to send you login codes by WhatsApp or email. We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with third parties except the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for storage and authentication, WhatsApp/email delivery for login codes, Google for Google sign-in).

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we use your Google account email only to identify your account. We do not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Storage and security

Data is stored on your device and in a cloud database where each account can read only its own records. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Your choices

You can log out at any time. To delete your account and all stored data, email us at the address below and we will delete it.
