Thekedaar App

Thekedaar App is a mobile app for construction contractors in Pakistan. It helps manage workers, daily attendance, advances, weekly (Thursday) settlements, projects, measurements and bills, in Urdu and English, and works offline.

Contractors sign in with their WhatsApp number. Email or Google can be linked as a backup way to sign in.
